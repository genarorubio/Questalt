📍 About QuestATL: Downtown Lawrenceville & Gwinnett Outdoor Trails

Interactive Walking Scavenger Hunts in Gwinnett County, GA

Looking for fun things to do in Downtown Lawrenceville, Georgia? QuestATL.com turns historic city squares into live, mobile-guided outdoor adventure trails. Start at the Lawrenceville Historic Courthouse Square, solve 9 real-world riddles along Croghan Street, Perry Street, Pike Street, and the Lawrenceville Lawn, and unlock exclusive VIP discounts at local partner coffee shops, bookstores, restaurants, and tasting rooms.

Trail Highlights: 1.1-mile walking loop • 35–50 minutes • Free to play on any smartphone • Dog & stroller friendly • Expanding soon to Downtown Duluth and Suwanee Town Center.
